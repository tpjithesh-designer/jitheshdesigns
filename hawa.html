The Direction

Based on the survey conducted, it was concluded that users get frustrated when it comes to ordering and doing their laundry, with 87% agreeing that it is actually the biggest frustration. I started working on this direction to make the experience easy to use, which will make people use this service more.

Preliminary Brainstorm

1.

What is the target market?

Our target audience is focused mostly on bachelors who are doing a job.

2.

Who else do we do business with?

We are doing business with detergent companies & machineries.

3.

What would they expect to find and interact with when interacting with our brand?

An easy interface to book the laundry service.

4.

What would we want them to see, that they may not have expected?

Quickly place an order with a clean user flow.

Problem Analysis

People don't have time to do it.

It's a time-consuming thing.

An app which can remind them to get their laundry done.
