Website Redesign

Imagility Website Redesign

Segmenting User Journeys for Higher Conversions

Imagility is a cloud-based immigration management platform serving law firms, companies, and individual applicants. The original website spoke to all three audiences through a single generic experience, resulting in low lead conversions and poor segmentation. I led the UX & content strategy for a complete website renovation — transforming a general-purpose site into three clear, conversion-optimized user journeys.

Client
Imagility — Immigration Management SaaS
My Role
UX Strategy, Information Architecture, Content Direction, Wireframing, Website Experience Design
Outcome
3× improvement in conversions, with clear segmentation and measurable ROI across all user groups
The Problem

One Size Fits None

Imagility serves three fundamentally different users — 🧑‍⚖️ Attorneys, 🏢 Companies, and 👤 Individuals. Before the redesign, the site forced all three through the same undifferentiated pages.

Key Pain Points

  • Overloaded content — too much information with no structure
  • Ambiguous messaging — users couldn't identify how Imagility helped them
  • Navigation friction — 4–7 clicks to find relevant content
  • Lead quality drop — the same generic funnel served every audience
  • No traceability — marketing couldn't attribute leads to a user type

✋ The Core Insight

A single unified website created competing priorities, diluted messaging, and a poor conversion ecosystem. Users needed clarity, not more content.

Research

Understanding the Users

Stakeholder discussions and heuristic analysis surfaced very different expectations across the three personas.

🧑‍⚖️ Attorneys / Law Firms

Needs
  • A reliable system to manage immigration cases efficiently
  • Clear visibility into petition progress, compliance, and documentation
  • Tools that reduce repetitive tasks and automate form generation
  • A secure platform to collaborate with clients and staff
Pain Points

Too much generic copy; unclear differentiation.

🏢 Companies / Employers

Needs
  • A centralized platform to track employee visa statuses
  • Streamlined workflows for hiring and managing foreign talent
  • Accurate, real-time compliance updates and reminders
  • A scalable solution for HR, legal, and operations teams to collaborate
Pain Points

Difficulty locating enterprise-level capabilities.

👤 Individuals / Applicants

Needs
  • Simple, step-by-step guidance for visa petition applications
  • Transparent visibility into case progress and required tasks
  • Easy-to-understand instructions without legal complexity
  • A trustworthy system to upload documents and communicate with attorneys/companies
Pain Points

Overwhelmed by industry jargon and multi-layered pages.

UX Audit

Audit Findings — What Was Broken

  • Siloed content living in a unified environment — creating confusion and loss of message relevance
  • Hard-to-scan content hierarchy — walls of text with minimal differentiation
  • Generic CTAs — "Learn More" everywhere meant no funnel direction
  • Unclear primary path — navigation presented mixed resources for every segment
  • Analytics blind spot — the team couldn't trace who was converting, or from where

Old Navigation (Across the Website)

Law Firm Company Individual Products Resources News Login Sign Up Request Demo

New approach — clear, role-based CTAs placed in the visible area.

The Strategy

Segmentation by Design

To solve the overload and lack of specificity, I redesigned Imagility's website into three sub-sites, each dedicated to a unique user type.

/attorney — For immigration law firms

  • OCR-enabled data extraction
  • Bulk upload
  • Immigration wizard
  • Immigration lifecycle
  • LCA / petition building
  • Petition vs. forms
  • Analyze petitions
  • LawPay integration

/company — For enterprises hiring foreign talent

  • Immigration
  • HR
  • Compliance
  • Talent acquisition
  • Company resources
  • Login / Sign up
  • Request demo

/individual — For personal applicants

  • Immigration
  • Family sponsorships
  • Digital user data creation
  • Petition building
  • Visas we cover
  • Mobile apps
  • Individual resources

🎯 Primary Goals of Segmentation

  • Deliver relevant information instantly
  • Build clear conversion funnels
  • Improve lead attribution & qualification
  • Reduce cognitive load & bounce rate
  • Strengthen SEO through focused, dedicated content
